Why 90% of AI Shorts Look Amateur (And How to Fix It)

You've probably seen it — an AI short that, if you look closely for 3 seconds, instantly feels off. Flat light. Plasticky faces. Static framing. Here's why, and the exact fix.

The 4 tells of amateur AI footage

  • Flat lighting — even, boring, no shadow. Real cinema lives on dramatic light (rim light, golden hour, candlelight).
  • No camera movement — static framing feels like a slideshow. Push in, dolly, or a subtle drift.
  • Generic color — washed-out grey. Apply a grade (teal-orange, crimson-gold, muted) for identity.
  • No emotional micro-expression — faces look blank because the prompt never asks for FEELING.

The fix: build feeling into every prompt

Cinematic close-up, a woman's trembling hand hesitating at a door, shallow depth of field, warm golden-hour backlight, film grain, subtle fear in her eyes, slow dolly push-in, muted teal-and-cream grade.

Notice: subject, shot size, lighting, grade, texture, emotion, movement — all seven. That's the difference.
