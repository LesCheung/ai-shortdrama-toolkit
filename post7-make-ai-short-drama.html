How to Make an AI Short Drama From Scratch (2026)

AI short drama is the fastest-growing short-form format in 2026. Here is the exact 6-step pipeline that turns an idea into a finished, watchable short — without a film crew.

Step 1 — Nail the hook (the first 3 seconds)

The hook decides everything. Write 3–5 opening lines that create instant tension or a reversal the viewer must resolve. If the first 3 seconds don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.

Step 2 — Write a tight script

Keep it to 30–60 seconds. Structure: hook → setup → conflict → small climax → payoff → one specific CTA. Cut every line that doesn't move the story. Short drama is the art of leaving things out.

Step 3 — Build cinematic prompts

Write per-shot prompts with the 8-dimension framework: subject, shot size, framing, lighting, color grade, texture, emotion, movement. Vague prompts = cheap AI look. Specific prompts = cinematic.

Step 4 — Generate the shots

One prompt per shot. Generate alternates, pick the cleanest. Keep a consistent lighting and color grade across shots so it feels like one film, not a scrapbook.

Step 5 — Edit for rhythm

Cut on the beat. Shots should be short and purposeful. Add subtle motion or holds where you want tension. Keep the audio clean and the pace tight.

Step 6 — Package and publish

Pair the short with a CTR-optimized title and thumbnail. Post at a consistent time, tie it to a related long video, and end with a specific CTA that sends viewers somewhere useful.
