How to Write an AI Short-Drama Prompt (Full Guide 2026)

The biggest bottleneck for AI short-drama creators isn't the tool — it's the prompt. A great prompt is the difference between "cinematic" and "cheap AI slop".

Why 90% of AI shorts look amateur

Most people write prompts like: "a dog running in the park." That produces a blurry, flat-lit, emotionless frame — instantly recognizable as AI.

The 8-dimension framework

  • Subject — who, wearing what, doing what
  • Shot size — WIDE / MEDIUM / CLOSE-UP / over-the-shoulder
  • Framing — 3rd person, eye-level, low angle
  • Lighting — golden hour, cold blue, candlelight, backlight
  • Color grade — crimson/gold, muted, teal-orange
  • Texture — 35mm film grain, cinematic, shallow depth of field
  • Emotion — tension, alienation, warmth
  • Movement — slow dolly push, sudden freeze, lingering gaze

3 ready-to-use templates

Revenge scene

Cinematic close-up of a muddy hand bursting out of fresh grave soil at night, lightning flash, atmosphere of betrayal and rage, 35mm film grain, high contrast, dramatic rim lighting, moody, emotional.

Secret identity scene

A refined villain in an opulent dark palace, slow turn, cold calculating smile, candlelight, crimson and gold palette, cinematic over-the-shoulder shot, shallow depth of field, rich detail.

Supernatural horror scene

Long dim hallway with an extra door at the end, flickering light, eerie atmosphere, slow dolly shot, supernatural horror grade, cold blue palette, film grain, unsettling.
