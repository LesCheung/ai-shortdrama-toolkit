The 6-Beat Storyboard for a 60-Second Short

The difference between a short that gets watched to the end and one that gets swiped: structure. Use this 6-beat rhythm.

The 6 beats

  • 1. Hook (0-3s) — the attention grabber. A line that creates tension ("I gave you everything. You buried me alive.").
  • 2. Setup (3-10s) — who/what/where in one breath.
  • 3. Conflict (10-25s) — the turn. Something breaks.
  • 4. Climax (25-45s) — the emotional peak. Highest stakes.
  • 5. Payoff (45-58s) — the reveal / twist / resolution.
  • 6. CTA (58-60s) — "Follow for part 2" / "Full episode in the link".

Why the first 3 seconds decide everything

On Shorts/TikTok/Reels, a stranger gives you almost no time. A strong Hook with emotional tension + reversal is what stops the scroll. Everything else supports it.
